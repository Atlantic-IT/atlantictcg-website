We Buy Cards

Looking to Sell Your Trading Cards?

Atlantic TCG is interested in buying trading card collections, individual valuable cards, and small binders. Whether you're downsizing your collection, leaving a game, or simply looking to sell cards you no longer need, we'd love to hear from you!

Contact Me

Games We're Interested In

  • Magic: The Gathering
  • Pokémon TCG
  • Gundam Card Game
  • Other trading card collections, considered individually

We're especially interested in playable singles, valuable cards, and organized collections. We also consider bulk collections, but we can't guarantee a purchase.

How Selling Works

  1. 1

    Tell Us About Your Cards

    Email us information about your collection, including:

    • The trading card game
    • Approximate number of cards
    • General condition
    • Your location (city or town — not your full address)
    • Your asking price, if you have one
    • A short description of the cards and their rarities — notable cards, sets, holos, alt-arts, foils, or graded slabs
    • Photos of valuable cards, binders, or the collection
  2. 2

    We'll Review Your Collection

    Atlantic TCG reviews submissions based on card condition, market value, current demand, and inventory needs.

  3. 3

    Discuss an Offer

    If we're interested, we'll contact you to discuss a potential offer and arrange the next steps. Local transactions may be arranged on Long Island.

Contacting us doesn't guarantee an offer or purchase, and no sale is final until we've both agreed on terms.

Selling Your Cards — Frequently Asked Questions

Do you buy every collection?

No. Every submission is reviewed individually based on condition, market value, current demand, and our inventory needs. We consider bulk collections, but we can't guarantee a purchase.

Do I need to bring my cards to a store?

No. Atlantic TCG is an online retailer and doesn't have a walk-in storefront. If we're interested in your cards, we'll arrange the next steps by email, and local transactions may be arranged on Long Island.

How are offers decided?

We look at card condition, current market value, demand, and what we need in inventory. Contacting us doesn't guarantee an offer, and no sale is final until we've both agreed on terms.

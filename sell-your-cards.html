Frequently Asked Questions

Do you buy every collection?

No. Each submission is reviewed individually. We consider bulk collections too, but we can't guarantee a purchase.

Do I need to bring my cards to a store?

No. Atlantic TCG is an online retailer without a walk-in storefront. We handle next steps by email, and local transactions may be arranged on Long Island.

How are offers decided?

Offers are based on card condition, current market value, demand, and our inventory needs.

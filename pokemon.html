About the Pokémon Trading Card Game

First released in Japan in 1996 and published internationally by The Pokémon Company, the Pokémon TCG is the best-selling trading card game in the world. Its enduring appeal comes from serving two audiences at once: competitive players who battle in the Standard format at local leagues and official events, and collectors chasing full-art trainers, special illustration rares, and the nostalgia of the original 151.

Modern sets release several times a year, each bringing new mechanics and highly sought-after chase cards. Recent eras have leaned heavily into alternate-art and illustration-rare cards that showcase the franchise's artists — which has made sealed product especially popular, since every booster box is a mix of playable cards and potential collection centerpieces.

Whether you're ripping packs for the thrill, building a competitive deck, or completing a master set, Pokémon rewards every style of engagement with the hobby — which is exactly why it stays at the center of what we do.
